Overview of game logic:
In main function a garbage object will be created and any inital players
will be created and passed into the garbage object
    Each player will be assigned a turn number which is based
    on where they will be in the players vector.
    New players are added to the end of that vector.

The play game function will contain the game loop managing turns
It will first reshuffle or reconstruct then shuffle the drawPile, making sure it
contains 52 cards with no repeats or jokers. Then it will call takeTurn for the
first player which will print the status of the game for that player and let 
the player take a card from the draw pile. Then they can replace a card in
the position of it's rank or with a wild (Jack). If that card can be put in 
the spot of another card, then it can be put there as well until the player 
discards or cannot place the card anywhere, and that player will discard it.
The next player will also have the choice of taking the top of the face-up discard
pile. If the draw pile is empty, then all the cards in discard will be returned
to the draw pile and reshuffled (some cards will still be on the table).
This will repeat until a player has all cards face up in the correct place.
At that point the rest of the players get one more turn and all winners
will only be dealt 9 cards (full card amount - wins). Then the 
opportunity for adding or removing another player will be presented
and the status of all players will be printed and the next turn started.
At the end of the game (once at least one player reaches 0 cards/10 wins)
the winner(s) will be announced and the game ends. The main function can then call 
playGame to play again.
